# RFVD sensor board

Open **RFVD_SENSOR_BOARD.kicad_pro** in KiCad 10. This folder contains the CAD project, its required custom libraries and model assets, and three explanatory reports.

Read the reports in this order:

1. [Device report](docs/RFVD_DEVICE_REPORT.docx): operation, architecture, components, signal processing, recording, calibration and engineering validation.
2. [PCB layout](docs/RFVD_PCB_LAYOUT_RATIONALE.docx): component placement, layer stack, routing and return paths.
3. [EMC report](docs/RFVD_EMC_OPTIMIZATION_REPORT.docx): grounding, supply filtering, simulation results and remaining qualification work.

The documents contain their figures and supporting explanations. Manufacturer web citations remain available for further reading. They do not require the development workspace or its evidence folders.
