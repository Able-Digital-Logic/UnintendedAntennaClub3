  - Live data: U6's substitute had only 4 in stock at JLC; the originals have 4,495 (U6) and 1,571 (U5) at Mouser; C9/C11 are back to 960 at JLC.
- **Prices are compared per line:** JLC/LCSC, Mouser and DigiKey (`04_parts/tools/sourcing_compare.py`).
- **Generic passives move to JLC Basic parts** where the specification is equal or better (`04_parts/tools/basic_swap.py`).
  - The rule: never at EMC's expense; low impact means take it.

**JLC Standard PCBA facts (jlcpcb.com help, 2026-09-09)**
- Standard PCBA is required: double-sided, 0201.
- Fees:
  - setup $51.12 (double-sided);
  - stencil $16.42;
  - $0.0016 per joint;
  - $1.53 per unique part, basic or extended alike.
- Fewer unique lines saves $1.53 each.
- Minimum 70 x 70 mm with rails, so **the panel (P1-10) is mandatory**.
- Assembling 2 boards out of 5 PCBs is allowed.
- Resin-filled, capped via-in-pad is free on 6-20 layers. Use 0.2 mm drill / 0.45 mm pad to avoid the small-via surcharge.
- US import is DDP, about 35% duty plus the Section 301 surcharge since 2026-07-24; no de-minimis.
