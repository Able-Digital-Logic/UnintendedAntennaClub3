# Critique status: every P0 and P1 finding (updated 2026-10-04, checkpoint 5 and the placement sign-off)

The critique of 2026-10-03 (19 lenses, every finding independently verified) produced 8 P0 items (critical or high, must be fixed before fab) and 32 P1 items (medium). The goal's first done-criterion: each is **fixed** or **documented** (with a reason and an owner). This table is the single place to check that.

Status words:
- **Done**: fixed and landed on the master, with the checkpoint named.
- **Built**: fixed in a gated scratch package, waiting to land with the named checkpoint.
- **Planned**: assigned to a later package with a defined fix.
- **Documented**: the finding needs no board change, or the decision and its reason are recorded.
- **Lead**: needs a lead action, listed in `LEAD_GUI_STEPS.md` or in the summary.

## P2 (low)

The P2 items are polish. Those that come free with the work above are taken there: mask 0, rule housekeeping, silk line width. The rest are listed in the critique synthesis, §2 "P2". Not done, by choice: footprint silk lines stay at the KiCad library's 0.12 mm (JLC's minimum is 0.15 mm). Widening them on the board would break library parity for about 300 footprints. Reference designators are hidden, polarity marks are also on the fab layers, and the CPL is checked in JLC's preview.
