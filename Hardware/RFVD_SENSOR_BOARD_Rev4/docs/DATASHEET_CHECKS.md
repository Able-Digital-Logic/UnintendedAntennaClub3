# Datasheet checks, Rev A

This file records that every IC and active part on the board has been checked against its manufacturer datasheet. Each row names:
- the document and revision;
- the page, figure or table used;
- what was compared;
- the result.

## How the checks were done
- **Critique, 2026-10-03.** One reviewer per schematic sheet checked each part against the datasheet:
  - every pin against the datasheet pin table;
  - the footprint against the recommended land pattern;
  - every value against the datasheet equations and the application circuit;
  - every rating against the worst case on this board.

  Two more reviews covered the whole board: a pin-map review of U8 against ST's pin database, DS12110 and the IBIS model, and a power-tree review of every rail. Every finding was then re-checked by an independent verifier.
- **Checkpoint 2, 2026-10-04.** Every value the simplifications changed was re-derived from the same datasheet (section 3).
- **Remaining parts.** Connectors J2/J10, inductor L301, fuses F1/F201, the B3U switches, the RN arrays and the ferrites were checked separately (section 4).
- **What the data is compared against.** Pins and nets come from `kicad-cli sch export netlist`. Pads come from the board file.

"Pass" means the item matches the datasheet. Findings carry their critique ID and say where they went (fixed in a checkpoint, firmware contract, lead decision).

The basis is checkpoint 2b (landed 2026-10-04, board sha256 `f539f0317c`). Rows that changed in 2b say so, and section 5 checks the parts 2b added.
